About Elite Kicks

Your trusted destination for authentic football gear — from grassroots to professional.

Who We Are

Elite Kicks was founded in 2026 by football fans, for football fans. We know how important the right equipment is — whether you're playing in a Sunday league or aiming for the top.

We started with a simple mission: to provide high-quality, authentic football products at fair prices, with expert advice and great customer service every step of the way.

Our Mission & Purpose

Our purpose is to make top-tier football equipment accessible to every player. We believe that better gear builds confidence and improves performance — and every player deserves that chance.

Our Core Values:

  • Integrity — 100% genuine products, always
  • Quality — only the best brands and latest models
  • Affordability — competitive prices and regular offers
  • Community — supporting local teams and young talent

Who We Serve — Target Audience

Our website is designed for anyone passionate about football, including:

  1. Youth players and junior teams looking for durable, reliable kit
  2. Adult amateur and Sunday league players
  3. Serious and semi-professional players seeking premium performance gear
  4. Fans wanting official merchandise from top clubs
  5. Parents buying safe, comfortable and long-lasting equipment for their children
