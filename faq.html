Everything you need to know about shopping with Elite Kicks.

We aim to dispatch all orders within 24 hours. Here are your delivery options:

  • Standard UK Delivery: 3–5 working days — £4.99 | FREE on orders over £75
  • Next-Day Delivery: Order before 2pm — £7.99
  • International Delivery: Available to selected European destinations — prices calculated at checkout
  • Click & Collect: Free collection from our Greenwich store

Common Questions

Q: Are your products authentic?

A: Yes — we are an authorised stockist for all brands we sell. Every item is 100% genuine and comes with full manufacturer warranty.

Q: What is your returns policy?

A: You can return any unused item within 28 days for a full refund or exchange. Items must be in original packaging with tags attached.

Q: How do I know my correct boot size?

A: We recommend measuring your foot length and checking our size guide in the product description. If you're between sizes, go for the larger size for comfort during play.

Q: Can I cancel or change my order?

A: You can cancel or make changes any time before it is dispatched. Contact us immediately by phone or email and we'll help you.

Returns Process

Follow these simple steps to return an item:

  1. Email us at returns@elitekicks.co.uk with your order number and items you wish to return
  2. Pack the item securely — include a note with your details
  3. Send to: Returns Department, Elite Kicks, 42 Football Way, Greenwich, SE10 8XX
  4. Once received and inspected, we'll process your refund within 48 hours
